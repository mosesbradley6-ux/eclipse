Frequently Asked Questions

Not sure if you need us? Here's what usually brings people to Eclipse.

My Wi-Fi keeps dropping or feels painfully slow — can you fix that?

Almost always, yes. It's usually router placement, outdated firmware, channel interference, or a network that's simply outgrown its setup. We diagnose it properly rather than just rebooting the router and hoping.

I'm getting constant pop-ups or a ransomware-style warning — what do I do?

Disconnect the device from Wi-Fi/network if you can, don't click anything on the warning, and contact us straight away. This is one of the few situations where speed genuinely matters — start a remote session or call us first.

How quickly can you actually help?

Remote sessions are usually live within minutes once connected. For anything requiring a visit or deeper diagnosis, we commit to a same-day response, Monday to Friday.

Do you only do once-off fixes, or ongoing support too?

Both. Some clients call us only when something breaks; others prefer an ongoing arrangement so we're proactively keeping an eye on things. Get in touch and we'll figure out what makes sense for you.

I'm really not techy — will I understand what you're doing?

That's the point of plain-English support. We explain what went wrong and what we did about it, without the jargon, whether you're troubleshooting solo or want us to just handle it.

Do you work with businesses outside standard office IT — like vessels in port?

Yes — see our Maritime Solutions above for network installations, intercoms, VoIP, CCTV, security systems, and TVRO/VSAT support for vessels visiting Cape Town.

Supporting your IT means we sometimes access your devices, networks, and systems directly.
